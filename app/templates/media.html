This is what plays when someone presses Play on a notice. Accepted: {{ clip_exts }}. Max {{ max_mb }} MB (change with MAX_UPLOAD_MB in the compose file). Keep clips short; they're copied into your Movies folder each time a notice is activated. Plex plays mp4 (H.264/AAC) directly on almost everything; other formats may be transcoded, which is fine.

Where are these stored, and what happens when I delete one?
Clips live in /data/clips inside the container (your plex-notice-data folder on the host), not in your Movies library, so Plex never sees them until a notice is activated. A clip used by a preset can't be deleted until you change that preset. Deleting a clip doesn't affect a notice that's already live, because that notice uses its own copy.
